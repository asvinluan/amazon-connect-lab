Lab Line: opening hours

The Lab Line is a demonstration contact centre built for a technical lab. It is not a real business.

Opening hours are 8am to 6pm Australian Eastern Standard Time (AEST), Monday to Friday.
The Lab Line is closed on weekends and on public holidays.
Outside opening hours, callers hear a recorded message and can ask for a callback.
The opening hours are the same for every caller, in every state.
