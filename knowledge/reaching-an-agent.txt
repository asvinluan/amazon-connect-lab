Lab Line: how to reach an agent

To speak to an agent, press 0 at any time during the call, or stay on the line. The call is then placed in the general queue.
Agents answer calls in the order they arrive.
If no agent is available, the caller hears a short message and stays in the queue.
The general queue holds up to 5 callers. When the queue is full, new callers hear a message asking them to call back later.
